# Ingredients
### Main
- Pasta noodles
- 100ml Vegan milk
- 3 slices Soft cheese (Weichkäse)
- 1/3 Lime
- 1 clove Garlic
- Parsley
- Soy Sauce
- Corn starch
- Oregano
- Pepper
- Fresh chilies

# Procedure
### Main
- Cook the noodles

### Sauce
- Cut garlic into slices, fry them in a saucepan/pot slightly
- Pour in the vegan milk
- Cut the cheese and drop it into the sauce
- Add spices (soy sauce, oregano, pepper, chilies)
- Add some pasta water. If sauce is not thick enough, add some corn starch water
- Add the noodles and mix it with the sauce
- Add lime and parsley

# Eat
- Eat
