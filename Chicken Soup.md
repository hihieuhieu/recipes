# Ingredients
### Main
- 3 Chicken Schenkel
- 1/2 Chinakohl
- 2 Carrots
- 1 Onion
- 3 Slices Ginger
- Chili
- Salt

### Sauce
- Green onion
- Grated garlic
- Chili flakes
- Sugar
- Hot oil
- Soy sauce
- Rice vinegar

# Procedure
### Main
- Cut Chinakohl, carrots, ginger, onions
- Put them into a pot (Order: Chinakohl, carrots, onions, ginger, chili)
- Pour some salt
- Rub chicken with salt, put it on top of the veggies
- Put a lid, let it cook (without water!) at low temperature for 1 hour
- Get rid of the bones from the chicken

### Sauce
- Mix green onion, garlic, chili flakes, sugar together
- Pour hot oil on top
- Add soy sauce and rice vinegar to taste

# Eat
- with rice or so
