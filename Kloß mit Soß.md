# Ingredients
### Main
#### Kloß
- Kartoffelkloßteigpackung
- Bread

#### Soß
- 1 shallot
- 1 tsp veggie broth powder
- 1 tsp mushroom powder
- Soy sauce
- Oregano
- Red wine
- Parsley
- Honey

#### Veggies
- E.g. beetroot (rote bete), red cabbage

#### Mains
- E.g. (veggie) bullette, schnitzel, etc

# Procedure
### Main
#### Kloß
- Cut bread into cubicles
- Fill Kartoffelkloßteig with bread cubicles and form them into the shape of a ball
- Boil water+salt
- Once boiling, drop the brotgefüllte Kartoffelkloßteigbälle into the water
- Let boil for 1-2 minutes, decrease heat to just simmering
- Let simmer for like 25 minutes

#### Soß
- Cut shallot, fry in a saucepan
- Add veggie broth and mushroom powder
- Add soy sauce, water
- Add oregano, red wine, honey, parsley
- Some starchy water (e.g. from Kartoffelkloßteig)

#### Veggies
- Cut and prepare

#### Mains
- Prepare

- Eat
