# Ingredients
### Main
- Green beans
- Minced meat / veg. alternative
- onion, garlic
- Tomato paste (Tomatenmark)
- Curcuma, pepper, salt, cinnamon, safran
- Basmati rice
- Butter, oil

# Procedure
### Main
- Cut beans into 2cm chunks, cook them in water for 6 mins. Drain
- Sauté onions in oil, add garlic and brown them for short
- Add minced meat / veg. alternative
- Add tomato paste, curcuma, pepper, salt, cinammon, stir well, add some water
- Dissolve safran in some water and add together with the beans to the pan
- Precook the rice until almost finished
- Butter and oil into a pot, alternating layers of rice and ingredient into the pot
- Top up with butter, pinch some holes into the layers and cover with a cloth
- Cook at low heat until finished
